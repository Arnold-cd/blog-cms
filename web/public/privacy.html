Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

🛡️

100% Privacy by Design

SLLK does not require user accounts, does not collect personal identifiers, does not track your reading activity, and does not sell or share data with any advertising network.

01

Introduction

SLLK is a clean, non-profit digital publication and e-library application dedicated to the study, transliteration, and preservation of indigenous Manipuri Puya manuscripts, literature, and educational essays in both Meetei Mayek and English scripts.

We believe that cultural reading should be private, serene, and respectful. This Privacy Policy transparently outlines our data practices for the SLLK mobile app across iOS and Android.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

SLLK operates with strict zero-data collection standards:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, provide an email address, phone number, or create passwords.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, addresses, contacts, or device hardware identifiers (IMEI/MAC).
  • No Location Data: We never request access to GPS, cellular triangulation, or IP location services.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The application contains zero advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or commercial telemetry brokers.
03

Local Storage & Device Permissions

To ensure high performance and enable offline reading in remote areas, SLLK stores selected data strictly on your local device:

  • Reader Preferences: Your selected article font size, reading paper tone, and audio narration speed are saved locally in private app storage.
  • Offline PDFs: When you tap "Download", the requested manuscript PDF is saved in your device's sandboxed document directory for offline reading.
  • Cached Articles: Downloaded article text is cached locally so you can read without an active internet connection.

This information stays exclusively on your physical device. You can purge all cached content at any time via Settings > Storage > Clear All Offline Cache.

04

Network Connections

The application connects to our verified Content Delivery Network (CDN) and GitHub repository solely to fetch public article manifests (posts.json) and open digital library documents (library.json).

These network requests transmit standard HTTP client headers (such as User-Agent) necessary to deliver requested media files. No profiling or cross-session tracking is performed.

05

Children's Privacy

SLLK provides cultural, linguistic, and historical educational content suitable for all age groups. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13.

06

Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal compliance. Any revisions will be published immediately at this URL with an updated effective date.
