' CHANU AMADI 'NINGOL

> Chanu nattraga Ningol hairiba wahei ani Asige khetnaba

Ningol nattraga Ningon (Tasengba hourakpham waheidi Ning-On nattraga Ningol) haibani. 
Echam chamna wahei asige wahanthoksi ayamba meenadi khangdabasu yaori. 

Nupi amana mama mapa gi mayumda poklaga, chaoba sangba mapung faraktuna mayum panba yarabasu pandaduna leiba matam asibu Ning-on   Ningol, hairaga khangnei. 
Ningolge wahanthokti -Ning-on, Ningol haibadi Ning-on haibani    Ning-on =Ning+On, Ning' Haibadi Ningba, khanba, 'On' haibana 'Onba' ' Oikhiba' haibani. 
Wahei asina fongdoklibade Nucha ( Nuja) Nupicha (Nupi oina poklabi) mahakna panthouge sangkaida chaoraklaga, mahakna nungsiba pamjaba sagei kanglup amagi nupa amada yumnak sagei onduna mayum pankhiba yai hairibani.  'Ningba matung enna onkhiba yai hairibani.

Atoppa wafam amana, mayum panlurabi nupi amana, nupa nupi chalnadabadagi nattraga karigumba maram amadagi yumpal punsi kaiduna, panthouge sangkaida amuk hanlaktuna ningol oina leiba yai. 
Kanagumba nupa amana   maga saman lengmalnaba nupi ama thengnare, nupi mahakpu nungsi pamjaba poknare. Adubu nupicha mahakpudi ningol oina leiribra nattraga sagei amagi moura haibadi khangba ngamdre ' fibam asida nupa aduna nupi aduda 'Ebemma nahakpu ningol la moura khangjaba yagadra? Hangliba wahang asi nupi aduna yamna munna khangani, nupi  aduna 'Ningol oina leijare' nattraga eide yum panlabini, sagei onlabini, haina khumlaknk. 

Ningol haiba wahei asige yamna naknaba wahei amana ' Chnu ' nattraga Chanu') haiba wahei asini. Sagei amada mama mapa amana pokpa nupi, mamombu 'chanu' kou- e. Chanu gi wahanthokti Cha+Nu (Cha+Nu) ,  'Cha' haibadi macha, angang, nupi oina poklabi angang amabu kangleicha meetei singna 
' Chanu'  hairaga khangnei, chaoba sangba mapung faraklaba matamda, Chanu amana sagei amagi nupa amada mayum panlaga 'Mou' 'Leima' Oikhi.
