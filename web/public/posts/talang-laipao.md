>Talang Laipao Haiba Wahei Anise Meetei Kangleichasing ge Khuwaidagi Senglaba Puya Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok da Yamna Sengna Eduna Leiba Wahei Concept Singni. 


Mathak ta Maming Pankhraba Puya asida Asumna Thainagi Kanglei Epa Epu Singna Eduna Thambiram e 'Lainingthou Salailal Sitapa Kee Mafam ta Talang Laipao Pituna Numit Hum ni Supaki Angang ke Matam Kumna Sanaki Masak Malpa Nouwa ꯱ꯦ ama Oina leilamke Ei asipu Nahak ki Nachamakni Ningtuna Eipu Yoklo Hayeng Chak Amata Taipang Palpa Manung Oinanapata Laiyamsing gi Ee. Yek Khonthok Matung Enna Mangtanapa Uli Wali Meena Chingpa na asi Kheipikpu Salaikpa Matamta Ei Nangki Mateng Pangkani haina Talang Laipao Pikhiye Leekhul Liklampu Laiyamsingna Tachakhi.
Eehan hanpata Tengpalpa Mapu Eepungloinapa Apakpa aduna malem Ema Leimalel Sitapi Leisathak asita Thouwai Palpasing Semnabagi damak Tengpalpa haibadi Numit Tha Thouwaimeechak Malem (Leisa) asina chingba kaya yaoba ahangpa Ating aa (Space) asibu semkhi. 

Mathak ta malem (Leisa) asigi mathak ta thouwai panbasing semnabagi wakhal tourakkhi matang asidadi Tengpalpa Mapu Eepung loinapa Apakpa aduna eehan hanna Taipang palpa Thouwai palbasing semlage haina 'Talang Laipao' pirakkhibani.
Mathak ki Quotation  da ahanba parengda yaoriba Lainingthou Salailal Sitapa hairiba adu eikhoina houjik 'Soraren Nattraga 'Epa Soraran haiba space asibu lai ama Oina Lainingthou Salailal Sitapa haina mingounei seithanei  
Laiyamsingna tachakhi hairiba laising aduna Laipungthou Talet puni loinana Salailal Sitapa suni. 


Mathak ta Puya dagi louthokpa wahei pareng sing Utkhrabada Talang Awangpa Ating aa dagi Tingpalpa, Tingpalpa Mapu aduna Eei malem da Uli Wali Meena chingpa pumnamak Semba laklage haina Talang Laipao pirak e  haibasina Taklibasi Taipang Palpa gi Thouwai Pumnamak asi malem leisadagi natte Nongmaton dagi lakpani haibasi yetnaning leitana thainagi Epa Epu singna khanglamle haibasi Yetnaning oiribra? Extra Terristrial Origin of Lifi haibasi kanglei Meetei Epa Epu singdi Cheihee Kayagi mamangda khanglamle haiba nattra? 
Mathang gi wafam amana 'Uli Wali Pumnamak semnaba 'Nouwa ꯱ꯦ ama Oina lakkani hairibasina Taklibadi Malem (Leisa) da leiriba Uli Wali, Sa, Nga, Pumnamak asi ꯱ꯦ ama haiba kangleicha Epa Epu singe Potsak Ama khaktagi oirakkhibani haiba natte? All Living Originated From the Seme Living (꯱ꯦ) of the Meetei Scripture (?). 


Talang Laipao haiba asi malem (Leisa) da Thouwai panba Uli, Wali Meena chingba pumnamak Mapu magi Ahanba Khongthang ni 
Wahei /Concept asiga masi leinaba kaya asina Kangleicha Thainagi Epa Epu singna Houjik ki matamda Science gi Wanglaba Thijinbadagi Fanglalibasing asi hanna Cheihee Lising Kayagi mamangda khanglamme. 
Hindu sina Puya Mei Thakhibadagi pumnamak asi Ut Onhankhibani.
