Khamlai (Laikham)  khamlai hyriba wahei asi 17suba chahicha faoba assam gee ,Ahom_ tai, ningthousingna latlamba , chahi hwba numita khurumi(khulumi) haiba laisang_mamangda hanuba laikham wahei asi haibada kangleichasingi lai aduni ngangbada haibada mathak makha onana haiba ngangbagi wani/ wahanthoksi kangleichsingi lai,        laikham" adu mathak makha onthoklaga oiba khamlai hairakpani "aroibada lai haibani/ englisha ta hairga,, the ulimate god haibani/ universal good father adubuni. 

PADMESHOWR GOGOI na iba the tai and the tai kingdom gi lamai 409,410 da asumna eri " kings flight and garhgaon occupied by mir jumla (17th march 1662)" He was acious to save from the england enemy raid the sacred articles of the two royal temples (deo_shals) of god kham _lai and god tai_kwphi. having taken into his  custody all the articles in them, he fish proceded to Raishat"

Eikhoina assam gi ahom_tai ningthwsingi puwarida ahom_tai ningthwsingna kangleicha meetei singi ahom tai ningthwsingna na kangleicha meetei singi lai ninglamba lamchat asi khngbadagi assm gee ahom_tai mi kangbu asi kangleicha meeteisingi lamdagi hwkhiba mi kanglupni haiba khng e / asia mhadesh ki mafam pumnamak tai haiba mi kanglup khuntaduna lei/
asia gi mafam pumnamakta tai haiba mi kanglup asi lee,loi,tai hairagasu khngnei/

Maram asina assam gee ahom_tai haiba mee kanglup  asi kangleipak kwbru(kwpulu)chingdondagi hwkhiba lai,loi haiba mee kanglup adugi manungda challi haibasi tasengna khangi
