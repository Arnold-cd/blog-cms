> Wakchingi Nongma Panba Chahi houba Numit ta Lainingthou Sanamahi da Katkadaba Potlamsing


-  Lainingthou Sanamahi da athenpot ama kabiyu.

-  Katkadouripa potlam khudingmak mei changdaba oigadabani.

-  Numitsida katpa yaba potlamsing di Lafoi Chang thokpa(haibadi masing 7,9,13,15 asinachingba), Hei oiragana ahum ntraga manga na chingba chang thokpa masingda katpiyu.

-  Katliba potlam pumnamak adu Lainingthou Sanamahi da hei natraga potlam adugi makhok aduna Lainingthou da onsinduna thamgadabani.

-  Meira 1 natraga 3 katpiyu.

-  Dhup Katpagi mathou tadye.

-  Numit asida eikhoina songadaba Laison thoidokpa karisu leitye toubatabu Lainingthou Sanamahi da eikhoina nada yektana punsi nungsangna panba, watta padaba oihanbiyu haiba asinachingba Nijagadabani.
  

## Lainingthou Sanamahee Khurumbagi Kanglon

1. Lainingthou Sanamahee da Nongma panbada (1,11,21-panba meeteithapan) ashida ATHENPOT  Hei-lei kattuna Angamba Kattuna Thounigani. 

 >Adubu Hindu laining changba kaya na Thasi numit 30 ni panba ashida ATHENPOT katkadabani haiduna kat-hanlam me houjiksu katnaba yaori. 
Mashibu "Laina meechaba kouee" Thasi numita Laingthou Sanamahi da ATHENPOT katlabadi imung adu mahi langgani leina tina chelakani awa ana tagani.

2. Lainingthou Sanamahi da hei lei angamba katpada Heibu masung taraga natraga machete katpa yade –mapum ngakta (mapum) katkadabani

3. katpagi maongdi esana pukchel khara sengna thajana katpiyu – Meira ama thalani, Hei lei mapum angamba khara. La da natraga achamba lai gi damak sijinaba amada haplaga katkani mei changba pottsu katnade--aduga KHURUMBA matamda khut lonaraga Khuramnade  khut anina Nijagadabani.

 > Lanjare haibagi Mawongda  khutsa tara lonlaga khuramba adu Nattaba. khoidou khada khut lonnaraga Khuramgani Thounigani. 

4. Pukham gumba ama sijinnaraga katpa matamda makhada la machet ama thagani aduga meira amadi meinam katpa matamda meira na Esagi yet ta thamgani.

5. Laphoi katpa matamda Longlaga Makok aduna Lai da onsinlaga Katkani.
