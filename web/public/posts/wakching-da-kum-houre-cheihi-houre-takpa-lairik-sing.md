Wakching Thada Kumhoure Cheihi Houre haibagi Puya amadi Lairik singda yaobiramba  khudam sing makhada pijari:

## 1. Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok Puya gi ahoubagi lamaida asumna eri,                    

“Puya asi ningthem pamheipa hakthakta sinthokpane, lepna khang-o. Ningthem pamheipa haktak phaopata, Maliyapham palcha cheihi kumsing lee cheising cheichat 3017 sulapane, cheihiki kumhou ahanpa numittaki sinthokpa houpana numit 15 ni changna wakchingki thaninta loiye.” 

Mathakki puya asigi wahanthokti, Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok Puya hairiba puya lairik asi ningthou Pamheiba matam maliyapham palcha kumsing 3107 suraba matamda sindokpani. Puya asi sindokpa asi wakchingki kumhou numittagi sindokpa houraga numit 15ni changna wakchinggi thanin numitta loire haibani. Masina takle kumhouba cheihi houba asi Wakching thada oire haiba asini.

## 2. Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok Puyagi manungda yaoriba wahei,                 

“Maliyapham Palchapu enga kumtam, engel kumkhaipa matam loutuna, masitaki kumchat singthalon laili laichat, numit, thasing, cheihi cheichat houkhiye.” 

Puya asigi waroldi, Enga kumtam, engel kumkhaipa matam loutuna  haibadi Wakching dagi Enga faoba tha 6pu kumtamle, Engel dagi Poinu faoba tha 6puna kumkhaire haibasini. Ngasi faoba enga kumtam, engel kumkhai haina hairi. Maram asi leibana wakching thada kumhoure haiba tak-e.

## 3. Tharon hairiba lairik asi Shree N. Ibhochouba na phongbani masigi lamai 42da asumna eri,                        

“Loitam kum yetoinana kumkhai tha, yoimayai taotalpana awang laina pangna numit yet oika masapi thacha pakhangpana oi oikumpa loitam wakching kumkhai cheihou thane haitapala” 

Mathakki wapham asina taklibadi, koirou numitpu makhalomda thoklambatagi kum ama loiraduna thadoklamlaga awang pangna chatkhatlakpabu (laiyum = uttrayan) yetoinana awang laipangna chatpabu tak-e. Awang lomda thoklamba thana cheihi loikhiduna anouba cheihi houba wakching thagi thadagi makhalom pangna lakpabu oioinana chatpabu tak-e. Numitna awang kaba thana makha pangna chatpa matamdi loidam wakching thani haiba tak-e. Maram asinasu wakching thada kumhou thani takli.

## 4. Tharon hairiba lairik asi Shree N. Ibhochouba na phongbani masigi lamai 42da asumna eri,                        
“Haona eton kang, tingkoi malai si, lailel meitingu ka yoipu masanouka, yoihei salai mangang ka……thacha wanintana wakching loulempa…….khanlei louta tha…….wapanbagi langkoi sayangthakta happi leima khunpi ulap pongma oipina langkoi sayangthakta tongna khanlei loukoilampa tha wakching leita thane”

Mathakki wahei asigi wahanthokti samna hairabada laisingnasu hanna touramba anouba cheihi amagi ehan hanna lou-upa houpa si wakching thane, leita thane hairi. Ngasi faobada moilang (moirang)da wakching thada yaifaba numit amada thangjing laigi thouram leipukta pangthok-e. numit asi bu moilang ta thangjing loutaba haina khangnei. Moirang da loutaba khoiba hounei. 
Wakching thada nong elik khikpa houre thangjing leitare phairen thada loutaba houjik loumee echin enao sing na pangthokli. Phairen thada chahi dugi afaba fattaba khangnanaba haiduna kum tamba haiba touribasi mapugi khutsemni. Kum tamba haibasi esing nungsitki kanglon ni.

## 5. Thaloi Nongkhailon haiba lairikta asumna eri madudi, 

''Chingu Sitapa khoiyum namungpana tengpanpa manung oinapa punung leptam loulake namu taipang oipapu safongke haina ningkhallatuna taipang nongsapanki thouwai ningsa panpa pumnamapu nongtam nongkhaike haipaki chingu khoiyum lainingthou yoipu khoimom ani mana liyetnana litinale. Chingu samu mahakchangpu pungamtalapaki khoimom lailelma leimalel sitapiti waana khangle waakching tha-o kou - ae''

Mathaki waafam asina fongdokliba wafamdi meeteigi meesemlonni. Thouwai panba taibang sina karigumba ama touge haina khallabadi kadai dagi hourakpage haibadu munna yengbiyu. Kadaidagi hourak-e. Koktagi haibadi english ta brain dagi hana houraklaga mathang mathang houkhi sandokhi. wakching haibadi waak + ching = wakon ie. kok + chingsinba / chingkhatpa haibani.

Mathakki wafam sina taklibadi meeteigi meesemlonda waakching haiba ashi na ahanba pumnamakki hourakfam adu oibana masina cheihi houfam thokle. Aduna sajibu thada cheihi houba natte haibadu mathaki puya ani asidagi eikhoina khangle.

## 6. Thalon Seknaipa puyada asumna eri,

“Langhei kumhou tha ahanpa, Loitam Wakching tha koupati keitou nongkino hanglapati, kum amataki, atoppa kum amata, chingtuna chatle haipakipu, wangmaching thane hainapane, wangmapu wak hainasu kouneiye, atu nongki wangma chingtaki wakching oikhipane, wakchingpu wangma ching koupati, panthou mapaki, machi oipa pumnamapu, panthou oipa maki, wakonpungta, wangna chingkhattuna thamme haipakisu, wakchingpu wangmaching-o touna kouye, wakching thapu, loitam chiktham tha hainasu kounapane, ………”

Mathakki wafam asinasu takli madudi, ahanba tha wakching asida kumhoure haibasi. Mapa oibada tha 2 naopuduna mama oibina tha 10 naopubagi wafamni.

## 7. Ahal Yathang kouba lairik Laininghal Naoria phoolo gi lairikta Saion 10 haiba tangkak ama yaori maduda asumna eri, 

“Khurumjari apokpabu
Lainingthou yaiphaba
Naongna natouna
Tengbanba oiriba!
Lamda tha oibana
Kharam lai phaorakle
Uri upalgi
Nanousu hullakle
Mapugi yathangna
Leirangsu satkhare.
Leiranggi mayondi
Naobipham oigare.
Ebungo lainingthou 
Nayathang chuthare
Apokpa maparel
Khudam phaorakle.”

Mathakki wapham sina taklibadi, lamta thana ahouba oidana poinu tha faobada saion 10 matangpalli. Epal panthou epada tha 2ni haibadi wakching amadi fairen tha asi leiramlaga lamta thada emada elik aduna thabu 10 naopubagi matang tak-e haibadi wakching asina thasinggi makokni haibasini.

## 8. Kanglei ningthou chahi haiba Shree Ngariyambam Kulachandra Pandit gi leimai 5 da asumna iri 

“Nongta lailel pakhangpaki mathakta pankhipa leinung loncha alipana palem panthou anina yangtoupi yangtoupa koun kumcha sakhipagi leithong phatpati kangla leithongta  phattuna thounilammi. Mathangta kumsingpu kumonnare thachapu tha onnare haipaki tamak kongpata leithong phakhiye. 
La : 9(sachifu cheiraoba singsatpagi maong) meitingu nongta lailel pakhangpaki mathakta pankhipa leinung loncha alipa na…… kongpata leithong phatkhiye ’’

Mathakki warol asina hairibadi laigidi mamei taibang meegi pukok epu athouba pakhangba gi poknafam kanglada wakching thada leithong phatlammi. Mamei thangda meetei gi kumsing ariba maliyapham palcha kumsing toktuna sakakda kumsingda onthokhre. Wakching thada chahi hourambabu beishnap ka channanaba sajibu thada chahi houhanduna onnana pangthok khre haiba takli. Maliyapham palcha kumsinggi wakching cheichat amada cheihi houringeidagi kangada leithong phatlam mi, kumsing amasung tha hongduna onnana cheihi houkhibadagi kanglada leithong phat handaduna kongbada leithong phthankh haina mayek sengna lairik asina eri.

## 9. Kang sanaba gi matang da Meeteigi masana kang sanabada kangkhut anidu mangsok amasung phamenda famduna pal-oinei. Mangsok (awang) da famba kangkhutna hanna kang kappa hou-e. Phamen (makha) lomda famba kangkhutna kappa loiraga pal (maikei) onthoklaga mangsok lomda famba, kangkhutna phamen lomda famlasu amuk hanna kang kappa hou-e. Masina taklibadi numitna awang laiyum (uttrayan) da kaba matam da cheihi hougani haina takli.

## 10. Meetei epu epa singna hairamlaba 

amadi ngasigi mirolnasu hainariba wakching thada kei , kot sokfade, kafade. Wakching thada men urep yungheide, yum mana sinheide, fee mei chak-heide, wakching thada nong khajiktang oirabsu chudrabadi leibak adu leiheide, wakching thada ahanba chuba nonglikpu hakchangda teiyu haina hainaba kaya leiribani. Wakching tha youdringeida poinu thada leibaktugi fou na chingba maru marang pumnamak keida hapkadabani haina hainari amasung tousu tounari. Poinu thada kum amagi aroiba thani wakching tha yourabadi anouba cheihi houduna cheihi loisille haiba tak e. Maram kaya asi leibana wakching thana tha 12 gi manungda maru oiba tha ama oina lounaribasi tunggi echa esu singna thiraklabada langla langjin oinaba haiduna thambiramba takpirambani.

## 11. Ching gi echil enao sing gi
 kumhou cheihou tha asi wakching thada pangthoknei. (Kumhouba amadi Wakching Cheihouba haiba lairik anida panli)

## 12.  Pheiyeng sekmai
 amasung khul khara leiba epa eton singna wakching thagi matungda sirabadi, numit 6ni da yum kei sengthoklaga lanna thouram, mathou mangam na wakching tha lakpa ngaiduna mathang gi cheihigi wakching thada pangthok e. karigumba wakching thagi manungda siba amasung thouram toubagi numit leirabadi wakching thagi manungda lanna thouram, mathou mangamna chingba pangthok e. masina taklibadi wakching thana tha 12 ni gi makokni haiba takli. 

## 13. Meetei na ngasifaobada cheihi 

khuding gi lamta gi ahanba thangjada saloi (saroi) khangbagi thouram ama leptana pangthok lak li. Masibu lamtagi thangja ahanba kounei. Masina anouba cheihi hougrabanina cheihi adugi nungai yaifanaba cheihi ama oinanaba saloi singbu tanthokpagi thouramni.

## 14. THALON APOKLON PUYA da asuma eri:

"Loitam Wakching tha koupati thouwaimilen hanpa mapuna taipang nouwa ama meetamlake haipaki pa oipaki Yaipi elik kontaki Yaipi elikpu wakon kokta chingkhattuna thampilampa Loitam thacha maki koloipu Wakching tha kou-a. Asumtouna thouwai milen hanpa mapuna pa oipaki Yaipi elikpu wakon kokta chingkhattuna thampikhipa asipu khangnanapaki tamak Wakching thata keitaki oining phou khainate. Atuka Wakching thati thatalanithoiki thahanni. Kumhou thane. Pallomtasu Wakching thaki unna kuplapa leimaitaki nongchingkhattuna kumhou nongchupa thane. Atuna Wakching thata nongchutalapati leipak atu leiheite hainei. Asina takpati Wakching thata nongsoitana chukatapani haipani."




## Cheiraoba kanglei cheihi houba natte :

Houjik matamgi Kangleipak (manipur) asida meeyamna khangnariba sajibu cheiraoba haiba thouram asi kanglei cheihi houba natte. Meetei cheihi di waakching thadagi hou - e hainaba lei. Khudam oina yengshi madudi Puya Wakoklon heelel thilel salai ama - elon pukok kouba puyagi sin tok lamba chaopa hairiba mee asinna asumna eeri ''puya asi ningthem pamheipa haktakta sinthokpane, pamheipa ningthem haktak phaopata maliya pham palcha lee cheising chei chat 3107 sulapane|| cheihi ki kumhou ahanpataki sinthokpa houpana waakching ki thaninta loiye||''   

Mathtakki waapham asina phongdoklibadi meetei haiba mee kanglup asigi cheihi waakching gi nongma panbadagi hou-ee haibani. Maram aduna houjik khangnariba cheiraoba asidi chahi houba haiba adu natte mashidi meeteina hindu changlak ngeigi semjinba waaphamni. Hindu laining chaokhatnaba sasinnaba meeyan kangbuga meetei thou leitaba meegi khongchum thakpa kanglupna meetei leipakpu maangthokpani. Aduna maram oiraga ngashiphaoba meegi meenai oiba ngamlibani.  Aduga amukshu meetei ayiba lailikta haibadi ''Thaaloi Nongkhailon'' ta asumna eeri madudi '' Chingu Sitapa khoiyum namungpana tengpanpa manung oinapa punung leptam loulake namu taipang oipapu safongke haina ningkhallatuna taipang nongsapanki thouwai ningsa panpa pumnamapu nongtam nongkhaike haipaki chingu khoiyum lainingthou yoipu khoimom ani mana liyetnana litinale|| chingu samu mahakchangpu pungamtalapaki khoimom lailelma leimalel sitapiti waana khangle waakching tha - o kou - ae||''  

Mathaki waafam asina fongdokliba waafamdi meeteigi meeshemlonni. Thouwai panba taibang sina karigumba ama touge haina khallabadi kadai dagi hourakpage haibadu munna yengbiyu. kadaidagi hourak-e. koktagi haibadi english ta brain dagi hana houraklaga mathang mathang houkhi sandokhi.  waakching haibadi waak + ching = waakon ie. kok + chingsinba / chingkhatpa haibani.   

Mathakki waafam sina taklibadi meeteigi meeshemlonda waakching haiba ashi na ahanba pumnamakki hourakfam adu oibana masina chahi houfam thokle. Aduna sajibu thada cheihi houba natte haibadu mathaki puya ani asidagi eikhoina khangle.

Meetei Epa Epu singbu Meetei Hindu singna Mangthokle :

Cheiraoba haiba dharmagi thouramni haina chathanariba asida meetei epa epu singbu hindu kanglupna namthaduna thamnaba haiduna numit asida arem akhak pijaduna namduna mangthoknaba hotnari. Cheiraoba haiba numit asida laironni haiduna semjin sajinduna Liraba ariba laining sanamahi lichatki pukok pathangkok singbu nam haduna meekha ponnaba sinli ngasisu tokpa leitri. Masibu kayam kuina namduna thamkhigadage, kanglei echin enao sing wakhal lousing leibidriba karigino? Khanduna amuktang yengbiu?  Cheiraobagi laironni hairiba asida numit adugi thong thakpa singdu yumthong khudinggi thongalda thadoktuna tinmu laimu fattabasing da pijaribani haiduna asumna lairon sol ahanbada chabiu haina kanada pijei. Pijariba laising adugi maming singdi asini - 1.Lammaba Tummaba, 2.Eelam Sengba Tusengba, 3.Kumsana Kumliklai aduga makhoina chabagi lukhak singdu 1.Hannu Kokchao , 2.Hannu Laikham na chai. Panjakhriba Lai sing asida lukhak chariba lai asi atei kanna busu natte eikhoi meeteigi senglaba Sanamahi hairiba lainingthoubuni. Kannagumbana Dr. Konsam Manikchan Singh gi "Sanamahi Laihui" haiba lairik adugi page 86 ta amuktang mityeng tabiu. Sanamahi Laihui da Sanamahigi maming singgi marakta Haikhriba Hannu Kokchao amasung Hannu Laikham ashi Lainingthou Sanamahigi mamingni mahakki saionni. Aduna Sonliba laironsing asi semdoktrabadi eikhoi meeteising meegi meekha asum ponduna leiba tare.  Matou asumna Meetei singbu tangkak khuding, khongthang khuding da migi minai oiduna asum leiba taribra..... Lairon singsi kanana semlambano. Kanduna amuktang yengbiu. Epa epubusingbu migi minai migi makha thaduna thamlaga macha maai oiba eikhoina karamna khangsige. Meegi minai oiba pamjade mikha ponbasu yaningde. Aduna eikhoi kari tousige..Epa epusinggi maton lomhalashe tumlagumse malemda shak taklashe kangleicha.
