## ANGANG UNABA (ANGANG POKPA) 

Panthou epa da tha ani, palem ema da tha tara noupuduna leiramlaga, emagi noupifamda leiba yadaduna, mapu mana thindokpiraktuna taibang lamjao faore (pokle), hek pokpaga nouwa adu eru-esengthokpigani aduga nahei lailonna angang adu sengdokkani. Pokliba nouwa aduna nupa oirabadi khoiri naofam adu yumgi kha-thangba_ fammen lomda fumgani. Nupi oiragana yumgi awang thangbada mangsok lomda fumgani. Mamagi humni khomdin laktriba faoba atoppa meegi khomlang pithakfaroi. Poklakeba angang khudingmak mamagi khomlang tamak pithakkadabani. Mama, macha animak epan youdriba faoba panthou epagi masak oiba koilou numit ki masak uhanfade. 
Mama amasung angang gi phee subada esingda laksinduna sufade, sugadouriba phee adu khudengda hanna haplaga phee mathakta esing heiraga suhani, hairiba thouram asi pokpa numittagi houna epan tarukni faoba pangthokkani. Masina angang esingda tabanachingba thokkande. 

## EPAN (TARUKNI) THOURAM

Ayuk anganbada mama amasung macha animak eru esengthokkani loinana nahei lailon sonduna yum sengdokkani. Ayukta epan thabagi thouram amasung pitai khomtaibi ema gi thouram tougani pokliba angang adugi mapana nattraga mapa marigi(mari-matagi) nupa amana angang aduda mingkhei ha,hung,hei,hae,hing haina pibigani, amasung nouwa angang aduna punsi lomdriba faobada kougadaba mingthol adu thonbigani.
Ten Kappa:- Chingkheidagi houna tenchei amamam kaptuna meiram faobada tenchei mari kapkani, tenchei amana nongthak amasung leikharomda utlamlaga emungda pusingani. Ten kapkadaba meedi poklakeba angang gi mamaga pokminnaba mabung nattraga mapuwana kokyet yetlaga kapkadabani. Angang aduda sen pibanachingba toufade, fambanda thounibigani. Mathanggi numitta famban kaigani.

## CHAKUMBA (TAIBANG CHINJAK CHABA) 

Nupina pokpadagi tha manga subada aduga nupana tha taruk subada taibang chinjak chaba houba oina chakumgani. 
Mama mapa amasung chakumgadouriba angang eru-esengduna nahei laisonna sengdokkani angang gi pheejol honggani.
Lainingthou sanamahee makaifamda athenpot ama ngamu yaona thellaga thounijagani, maduna thong-thaklaga chakumbagi thou pangthokkani.
Athongba chak yensang adu magi magi yek salaigi pu ahumda luk ahum thokna katkani. 
(Pu ahum hybadi:- eputhou pakhangba, salai, salaina pokpa) 
Lukman khara khara luk ahum adudagi louraga makhagi asumna mamana enbigani. Chak enbada mam-mam, esing enbada ku-ku hygani. Asumna taretlak chak en tamgani.

## NAHUT-NARENG

Nahut-narenggi thouram hybasi meetei enatki oina tangai fadraba toudaba yadaba thouram ama oina leiramdabana maram oiduna na hutpa pambiba meeoina numit-tha achangba yaifaba numit amada pangthokpiba yai. Yarabadi pokpa numittagi numit taruknigi manungda pangthokpibasu akaiba leite thouram asibu enat ka mari leinaba achouba thouram ama oina pangthoklubagi mathou tade.
