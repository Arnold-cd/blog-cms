Kangleipak ki kha nongchupta awangba chingsang ama leiriba asibu kangleicha singna thangching kou e. Thangching haiba ching asi puyada maming panba, ahanba taipang leisemlakpada yaoba chingsang amani. masi epung loinapa apakpana malem leisa semlakpada yaorakpa Laipungthou taretki manungda ama oiriba chingsangni.

Thangching amasung thangching koilel lai sitapa haibasi khngminasi.

Pabiriba singgi mafamda ahanbda Aebana ningsingjaninbadi eikhoina moirang gi hindu matmgi mamang gi oiba esei, penana chingba taba matamda, eputhou thangching koilel lai, haina etat tattana aseibasing gi machundagi, aebasing gi khorjeidagi taba uba fang e. makhoina sakliba sonliba waheising asi tasengna puya da yaormliba waheising aduni. Matang asida eikhoina khanglibadi #Thangching haina eikhoina uriba chingchao asi tasengna schamba ching amadi natte, ching asigi maming matou karamna tengpalpa, tingpalpa semba mapu mahakna leinaramliba, karigidamak kloilel lai sitapa, kouramlibano haibasini. 

Puya pumnamk ki makok pukok oiriba #Wakoklon_heelel_thilel_salai_amaElon_pukok_puya, asi eba hourakpa matamda puya asida asumna eri 'Epung Loinapa Mapu Na Nungpung Wakhaltaki Malem Leisapu Enung Pungtaki Fukhat Chingkhat Thangkatlakpa Matamta Nungpung Wakhaltaki Thokkhipa. 

Mathaki puya dagi louthokpa wahei pareng sing asi munna pabiyu, mathang amuk puya da sumna eri, ꯱• Hailipa Mapu Na Talang Malang Oina Haina Humna leilingeita, Mapu Sitapa ki Nungpung Wakhal Na Enung Pungta Lupkhituna Malem Leisapu Fukhat, Chingkhat Thangkatlakpa Matamta Enung pungki Saion na, Kampi, Laicha, Malangpa Chongthokley. 

Anisuba puya dagi louthokpa wahei pareng asing adu munna pabiyu, ahum suba oina puya asumna eri.

Laipungthou Taletki Eyek Pathap kaitana Amati Nongpok chingkhei Apanpaney, Anisupana Awangpa Koupalu ney, Ahumti Thangching Koilel lai Sitapa ney  Talet ti Salailel Sitapa ney Talet na Laipungthou ney.

Mari suba oina puyada eriba asisu munna pabiyu yengbiyu. 

> Epung loinapa apakpna mathanata leipa yatatauna haina humlakpaki hung, hung ki ewai enin na niksinlakpata, Thenglupa atupu laiki masani haituna Laisa (leisa) koukhipa atupu enung pungtaki epung loinapa apakpana fukhat, chingkhat thangkatlakpata loinalakpa, amati leisapu thengnapataki thoklakpa, kampi, laicha, malangpa haina koukhipa. laipungthou talet ki laiming thontuna emai faokhipa, emai atupu laiki khutamni lai malley haituna malem haina koukhipa. taipang palpa ama oilaknapa epungloiki nungpung wakhaltaki thokhipa pumnakpu takpilakey khantuna amati thak ka, kha ka aniki malakta, laiki maheipu taipang mee oina palhalley, Atuki malamna taipangta palley haituna taipangpalpa koukhipa. 


Mathakki wakoklon heelel Thilel salai ama elon pukok puyadagi louthoklakpa asi munna pabiyu. 

Aebana mathakta pijakhiba puyadagi louthoklakpa marigi,  marida piriba eriba pumnamak munna hek khangbada kangleipak ki kha nongchupta ta leiriba chingchao, chingsang asi thangching haibasi malem asi semlakpada yaorakpa, khuwaidagi liraba, malem gi chingsang ni haibasi khanglagani. 

Cheihi leesing kayagi mamangdagi kangleicha meetei singna kangleipak ta puthoklamba leisemlon (theory of creation of the earth) gi matung enna puya na hairibadi eikhoina houjik U, waa uche kaya,  sa, nga, mee na chingba leesing kayana chngjafam louduna hingduna leiriba malem asi epung loinapa apakpa (the ultimate universal lord) aduna mathakta leipa yatatuna, leiningdadana hung, hung haina humalakpagi ewai enin na (makhon) aduna niksinlaklaga, famchinlakpadagi oikhibani haibani. tingpalpa epung loinapa apakpa epungo mahakna mahakki ewai enindagi famjinlakpa laisa (laigi masa) leisa (leipak) adubu enung pungtaki (mapu mahaki mapan naidraba, (Endless blood whole) dagi chingkhatlakhibani. 

Mahakna ahanba chingkhatlakpa laisa (leisa) adu malem leipak haina koue. Masi hindu singna prithibi, nongchuplomgi English kangbu Earth hairaga khangnei. 

Malem asi ahanbada epung loinapa apakpana masagi epung elon nungdagi chingkahtlakpa matmta mahaki masamak ni (eikhoi pumanmakpu semba lai mahaki masani haibadagi puyana) laisa hain koue. Laisa haibasi eikhoi pumnamkna uriba, sokliba malem leisa asini. Puya na takpa matung enna malem sembada yaorakpa lapungthou taret ki manungda ahum subani, thangching koilel lai sitapa" koue. 

#Thangching_chingchao asi mapu magi masana oiba laisa malembu mapu magi masamakni epungdagi chingkhatlkpa matamda ahumsuba oiriba awangba atongba oiba marmna mapu mahakmakni haiduna kangleicha meetei singna, Thangching koilel lai sitapa, haina mingkounei. Koilel lai sitapa haibadi nongthou Atingaa buni. Nongthou atingaa asi epung loinapa apakpa amahakki saion ni.
