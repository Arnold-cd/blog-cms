# Karigidamak saloi (Saroi) khangba twribano?

Lamtagi thangcha ahanba haibasi lamta gi ahanba thangcha haibani. Lamta gi Thangcha ahanba haibasi KANGLEICHA meeteisingi malemda leiriba thouwai panba pumnamak asi gi thouwai Yaipi ( Extra terristrial origin of Life ) Adu Nongmatonda gi lakpani haiba khudam (evidence) ni.

Tengpanpa Mapu mahakna taipang meeoiba Proto human ama sabada numit 7 ni Changi, Langmaiching numita gi houraga Thangcha Numitta loi. asumna Mapu mahakna saraba Proto human adubu PUYA na meekhaloupa Kou e. Meekhaloupa mayam asi thouwaimichak cheiyom 7 ta hapchinduna Ating-aa manungda da Thammi.

Asumna sem saduna Ating-aa da thamnaba, Proto human meekhaloupa adu Mapu mahakna Lamta gi Thangcha ahanba numitta Ema sakpi malem asida santoknaba ahanba oina tharakpa numit ni. Lamtai Thangcha ahanba gi saroi Khangbagi thouram asi malemgi meoipumbadagi Kangleipak ki salaicha meetei singna Sceince ki lamda mang tharambagi khudamni.

# Saroi (Saloi).

Kangleicha meetei eikhoina leiriba lam kangleipak asi mapu mahaki saktamni haiduna lounarmmi lunna  khanarami. Mapu mahakti nungshibadagi nungshibani fajabagi fajabani, achumbagi achumbani. Mahakki mafamda aranba, athiba faoba touba yade. Mapu mahakki aningbani. Meetei kangleicha eikhoigi thajabini.

Asumna mapu mahakna taipangcha eikhoibu semkhre, mahaki mami dagi semkhibani haibagibu mee koue. Mee haiba pot asi meeyoibana poklabanina, semlabanina 'sha, oibagi saktam amaga loinare, mee na khuwaidagi singba sha oijarabasu, meyoibagi masada matik mayai ani lei awangba thaki oiba mapu mahaki mask oiba ama (Rationality) aduga anemba thaki oiba shaga loinaba ama (Animality), matik ani asi punsinlaga meyoiba haibadu oii. 

Asumna meyoibagi masada yaoriba anembagi matik mayai adubu meetei kangleicha singna Saroi (saloi) kou-e.
Saloi haibadi Sa+loi haibani saga+loi loinaba, meeoibada yaoriba sa gi oiba sarak adubu haibani. English ta hairha Animality haibani.

# Saroi_haibasi_kamaina_oikhibano?

Saroi haibasi epung loinabagi humang mariktagi thokhibani. Humang, hu+ mang hu haibadi meetei na chabada siba nattrga fattaba thokpa potpu khangnei+ mang haibadi mangkhiba haibani. Hakchangdagi thokpa mahi makhal fattababu meeteisingna humang kou- e.
Karinungi humang kouribano hairabdi hakchangdagi mahi asina hu oiba sarukpu puthokhi manghankhi haibagibu humang koue. Maram asina Saloi (houjik matmgi waheina saroi), haiba kangbu asi mapu mahaki masada yaoba hu oiba fattaba asidagi oikhibani.

Puya gi oiba likhunda hairibadi, meeyoibada yaoriba afaba, fajaba mapu magi manakta nakpa matik mayai sing asibu laiyam koue aduga mee oibagi masada yaoriba fattaba sagi oiba saruk adubu saroi koue. Maram asina laiyambu haibadi afaba fajababu, saloi na haibadi fattaba chumdabana otpa ayetpa piba wahanba haibasi masna tabani. Afababu fattabana chingthanaba hotnaba haibasi taibangi hounabini. masibu laiyamna amasung saloi gi wari oina puthoktuna utpani.

# Nongshapa_cheilak. 

Nongshapa cheilak haiba waheising asi Wakoklon heelel thilel salai ama elon pukok puya da yamna sengna fajnna yaoba waheising, wakhalolsingni. 
Nongshapa cheilak haiba asi houjiki matamda hindu singna dandi, english ta punishment haiba wakhalol aduni.

Kangleipak ki awang nongchup ta leiriba koupalu (koubru) chingsang saruk amada tingpalpa mapu epungloinapa apakpana semba lai haiba mee kanglup aduna koupalu chingdonda, pak sanna nungai yaiphana panba hourakle. Koupalu chingdonda pallamba meeyoising asi nupa na lai, nupi na leima hairaga khangnarami. 

Houjik 21 suba chahicha asi faobada kanglei khunnaina, kanglei sana leimayol, awang koupalu asuppa leima lai khunda ahanba, awang koubru asuppa laiyam khunda ahanba haiba yamna ekai khumna lainingthou koupalu bu amasung mafam aduda taibangda ehan hanna khundaramba epa epu sing amasung ebensing adubu kangleicha singna seirol seithari, ningsingli amasung chahi khudingi mafam aduda khulumpa (khurumba) chatnari. kangleichasingi khunnaidi koupalu chindondagi hourakpani. 

Koupalu chingdonda leima lai hairiba mee kanglup ahanba asina matam sangna nungaina yaiphana pallakpa matam asida saloi fulup haiba kanglup amana ayetpa pirakle. leima lai singda ayetpa awaba pirakpa asi tingpalpa mapu epungloi apakpa aduna ubiraduna, epungloi apakpa mahakna saloi kayabu cheilak (punishment) pinabagidamak, #Nongshapa koupa saki saktam ama louduna saionlakle. nongsapa na saloi fulup tumna hatkhre. masibu kanglei khunaina ngasi faoba Nongshapa cheilak hairaga khangnei.
