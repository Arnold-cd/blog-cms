Laileibak ( Laileipak) Meetei singna 18 suba Cheihi chada Hindu ge Leechat , machat dharma lourubadage asengba Meetei Kangleicha ge Leechat Manat ( Customs and Traditional ) mangkhre .

Sak Khangdaba Meeoi kanglup amage mawong matou lourubadage asengba kangleicha singe 

Lonsu thadokkhre wahei kayage fajaraba wahanthok kaya magkhre 18 suba Cheihi chada chai kaya matang mathang kangleicha singe Lai singna thamlamba ayeba potsak Puya pumnamak meida tumna thadokkhibadage Epa Epu singe Cheihi lising kaya changna semgatlaklaba ayeba lairiksing pukei ( Royal Library) lousinge pukei mangkhre.

Wakhal hankhre chakcha yuthak hankhre sak chu onkhre Pakhangpa bu ( Snake ) ni haijinlakkhre lai gi macha Kanglei Meetei bu Lin gi machani haijinlakpasi kangleicha singbu hanthaba kangbuni hanthaba mawongda lourakpani haiba utnaba hotnarakpani pumnamak Mafam marang khangdana honglakliba wakhal paringda Meetei ge Mayek 18 Chatlambadage Bengali mayek 35/36 namduna chathankhibadage amuk hanna wahei ge pukei Mafam marang khanglaktre.

Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok puya  Tengpalpa, Tengpanba  amdi Taipangpalpa amadi Meeoiba semduna salai sakhai tarakpa faobage wahou nonghou puya ni .

Puya asi kangleicha Meetei ge khuwaidage liba amadi asengba puyani haiduna A.D . 2009 da Mee 100 rom tinba Kangleipak ki puya heiba Mee yangkheimuk yaoba Meefam amana lepkhrabni angakpani puya asida Laileipak amasung Lairamlen , amasung masiga Mannaba wahei wata amata mitna udaba Lai aduge , Mafam amasung Mafam manba wahei wata amata yaodaba asini.

Puya asina eikhoina houjik Atiya nattraga epa soraren haina sijinnariba wahei asi Aating-aa  Koilou sitapa, Salailel Sitapa haina khangnei masige waheising asi Taipang semba Mapu  Taipang gi mapa oiriba aduge Leifam mahak ki Mafam Lainingthou mahak ki Mafam tungnafam khangnaba waheising aduga amuk puyana " Koilou Nongmatonna Wangpala Panthou Epana Mawang e " hairibasida Nongmatondage Henna wang e hairiba laige lai oiriba Mapu ge Mapu oiriba Aruba lai panthou kou e.

Mahakna Talang malang na Eem ( Yum ) oina leiram e Puyana hairi hairiba Lainingthou Lai ge Lai oiriba Mahak ki Leifam tungnafam  singtha leitharakpada leireibak ( Laileipak) Lairamlen Lailamlel haina eramba hairamba amta kangleicha singe Ariba wahei pukeida leite ayebana Ejariba asi puya ge wafam khra hanna khangbiba pumnamakna yaobigani .

Mathak ki hainariba maram kayasi leinaba Laireibak (Laileipak ) Lairamlen (Lailamlel ) haiba waheising asi kangleicha singe Mapa Mapu oiriba Lai Nattraga Leima Lai haina khangnaba Koubru ( Koupru) dage hourakpa Meeoising Aduna leiramba taramba Kangleipak amasung Kangleipak ki mayon Kanglei tampak achouba asibu khangnaba waheising ni haina loi e.
