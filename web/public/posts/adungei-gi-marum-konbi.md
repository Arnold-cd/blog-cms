Meetei thapalon da tha amasi numit 30 nin na sai. Adunadi chahi amada 12x30=360 numitkhaktani. Adubu malem na numitki akoibada amurak mapung fana chatpada numit 365¼ changi. Maram asina thabangi chahidagi numitki chahina numit masing 5¼ (numit 5 ga Meetei pung 16) sangba helli. Chahi 12 gi Chak amada thabangi numit asi numitki khongchatta paba chahidagi numit 5¼ x 12 = 63 numit putthare.  Marum-Konbi sannabada nakal amamamda Laiyum 6, 6 leiduna punna Laiyum 12 ni. 

Nakal amamamgi khwaidagi yet thangba laiyum adubu Pi koui amasung Pi anida Marum 6, 6 happi loinana atoppa Laiyum 10 dudadi Marum 5, 5 dang happi. Sannaba houbada punna (6x2) + (5x10) = 62 marumni. 

> Aing-asa (season) di Numit (sun) dagi louba chahi gi matung illi. Aduna chahi 12 loibada thaban gi thana Numitki (solar) thadagi tha ani henna ngankhatna chatkhre. Masi chumthoktrabadi mahei marong thabada matam loina kairagani. Numit 360 nina saduna numitki chahidagi numit 5¼ putthahouba aduna marum oiraga, chahi kouba matamgi masak oiba komsing aduna mapi oiduna kollaga chahigi macha tha ollak-i haibagi masanna asibu Marum-Konbi koubani. 

Sannabagi maongdi, ahoubagi oinadi Pi anidu nattaba Laiyum amadagi marumsingdu loina loukhattuna yetlomgi yumsing aduda yenthokkani. Yetlomda yellibasi, malemna Numitki akoibada yetlomda (anticlockwise) chatli haiba taklibani. Yelliba marumsing adu loikhiba yum aduga hek thangnaba marumsing adu amuk loina loukhattuna hannagum yenthokkani. Karigumba nung loikhibada ahangba yum (kom) ama leirabadi maduga thangnaba marumsing adu chare haina loushingani. Masibu Si-kang-si koui.



 Mathangda atoppa sannaroi aduna amuk marum shingani. Nakal animakna angamba marum chanaba hotnagani. Marum shinba loibada karigumba ahangba kom amadagi henna leirabadi madu Nungkang koure, marum chaba yadre. 


> Chahi 12 da putthahouba numit masingdi 63 ni aduga sannabada sijinnariba marumdi 62 khaktani. Ningshingbiyu, sannaba loiba khudingi iroi loiba marum ama komda lemgani, madu Nungakang Yeinaba haina atoppa maru amana thomduna komdagi tankhatkani, aduga angambaduna lougani. Masina 63 sugadabada watkhiba marum amadu menkhatlibani.


Marum Loina channaraba matungda mathangi sannaba houbada nakal amana mahakki yum 6 makki Marum suna thangatpa ngamdrabadi, wat_houraba yum natraga yumsing adu atoppa sannaroi adugi Lum oikhragani. Lum di paikhattuna shinba yade, amasung maduda tariba marumsing adu Lum gi mapuduna loibada fangani. Mathang Marum amuk hapkatnaraga sannabada maru suna thangatpa ngamba yumsingidi Lum oiraroi aduga Lum asi masing 3 dagi henbasu yade. Karigumba nakal amana kakhal anidagi henna thangatpa ngamdrabadi nakal animak kakhal anikhaktang thangatnaraga sanagani amasung Lum su leiraroi, masibu Pi-Pa sannaba koui. Makha tarakpada Pi anidudang maru hapkatnaraga sannarabadi madu Pi-Pi sannaba koui. 


> Thaban gi chahi na Numitki chahidagi numit masing 5¼ sothahouba adu kaodanaba Meetei Ipa-Ipusingna Cheiraoba hek loibaga numit 5 ni Silhenba haina pangthoktuna Kang na chingba masanna kaya sannaduna lelli, maru oiba thabak amatta pangthoknaramde, numitki khongchatna youshillakpa ngairammi. Chahi mari (4) matung khuding Phailel (Phairel) thada ahenba numit masung (¼) mari adu punshinduna ahenba numit ama haptuna tha adu numit 31 na sagani, masibu Nungkang Phairen tha haina kounei amasung chahi adu numit masing 361 na saduna Nungkang chahi (Leap Year) koui. Chahi taruk (6) khudingi wat_houriba numit 5 sing adu punshinduna Phairel thada ahenba tha ama hapchingani, madubu Thalol (Tharon) natraga Amangba tha kougani, asumna 6 suba chahi du numit 360+30=390 na sagani. Chak adugi aroiba chahi, haibadi 12 suba chahi adusu Tharon ama amasung Nungkang numit ama hapchinduna 360+30+1=391 numitna sagani.

 Nungkang numit 3, tharon 2 matam chana hapchinduna  Chak amada thabanna sothahouriba numit 63 adu asumna menkhattuna chasinnahallami.
Asumna, masanna asi puthoktuna thak-wanglaba eikhoigi Ipa-Ipusingna naha singda numit amasung thagi tharon karamna pagani haiba khanghallami.



Miramdagi lakpa kayana thaina masanna asibu pamna pukhrabasu manungda chelliba kanglon adubu khangdabana komda maru marang thaba amasung lokpagi masanna oigani haina lounei, loinana makhoigi akaknaba kom masing asi Meeteina sannabaga mannade. Tasengbadi Marum Konbi komda maru marang hunba natte, masina neinaduna amadi puthoktuna taklibadi mahei marong thabagi chap chaba matam aduni.
