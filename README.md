# 📖 SLLK: Cultural Reader & Digital Publication

A modern digital publishing platform and cultural reader dedicated to the preservation, exploration, and global accessibility of indigenous Manipuri literature, historical chronicles (*Puyas*), philosophical essays, and classical archives.

Available across both the modern web and mobile platforms.

🌐 **Web Application**: [https://arnold-cd.github.io/blog-cms/](https://arnold-cd.github.io/blog-cms/)  
📱 **Android Application**: SLLK: Cultural Reader on Google Play

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📜 Cultural Literature & Historical Archives
- Digitized collections of foundational manuscripts, philosophical treatises, and cultural essays.
- Curated digital library of historical texts and research documents for academic study.

### ✍️ Authentic Script & Dual-Language Reading
- Native **Meetei Mayek** (ꯃꯤꯇꯩ ꯃꯌꯦꯛ) typography and full Unicode rendering.
- Integrated dual-script transliteration (Meetei Mayek / Romanized Latin) for scholars, students, and language learners.

### 📱 Cross-Platform Experience
- **Responsive Web Portal**: Clean typography, night/light reading modes, and real-time article exploration.
- **Native Mobile App**: Optimized for Android with offline caching, fast reader views, and seamless document browsing.

### ⚡ Serverless & High Performance
- Fast, secure, and resilient content delivery powered by modern edge and static CDN infrastructure.
- Zero invasive tracking with full respect for reader privacy.

---

## 📱 Mobile App (Android)

The companion mobile application provides:
- Distraction-free article and manuscript reading with custom typography.
- Offline support for reading in low-connectivity environments.
- High-contrast Dark and Light themes tailored for prolonged reading comfort.
- Built-in document viewer with fast navigation and page caching.
