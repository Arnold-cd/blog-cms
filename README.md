# 📖 SLLK: Cultural Reader & Digital Publication

A dedicated digital publishing platform and cultural reader focused on the preservation, exploration, and global accessibility of indigenous Manipuri literature, historical chronicles (*Puyas*), philosophical essays, and classical archives.

📱 **Mobile Application**: SLLK: Cultural Reader on Google Play

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📜 Cultural Literature & Historical Archives
- Digitized collections of foundational manuscripts, philosophical treatises, and cultural essays.
- Curated digital library of historical texts and research documents for academic study.

### ✍️ Authentic Script & Dual-Language Reading
- Native **Meetei Mayek** (ꯃꯤꯇꯩ ꯃꯌꯦꯛ) typography and full Unicode rendering.
- Integrated dual-script transliteration (Meetei Mayek / Romanized Latin) for scholars, students, and language learners.

### 📱 Immersive Mobile Experience
- **Optimized for Android**: Clean typography, distraction-free reading, and night/light reading modes.
- **Offline Reading**: Support for reading in low-connectivity environments.
- **Document Viewer**: High-quality document viewer with fast navigation and caching.

### ⚡ High Performance & Privacy
- Fast, secure, and resilient content delivery powered by modern CDN infrastructure.
- Zero invasive tracking with full respect for reader privacy.
