Official Policy

Privacy Policy for SLLK

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 • Version 1.0

Introduction & Commitment

SLLK ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to preserving, transliterating, and promoting ancient Manipuri Puya manuscripts and indigenous literature. We prioritize user privacy and believe that reading and cultural study should remain completely confidential.

Summary: SLLK does not collect personal identifiers, does not create user tracking profiles, does not serve targeted ads, and stores your bookmarks and reading preferences strictly offline on your own device.

Information We Collect

SLLK is built with privacy-by-design principles:

  • No Personal Information: We do not require account registration, email addresses, names, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Local Device Storage: Bookmarks, reading font size, theme preference, and downloaded PDF documents are stored entirely in your local device storage. They are never uploaded or synced to external profiling servers.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Narration uses your device's built-in native speech synthesis engine. No spoken audio or reader text is transmitted to our servers.
  • Network Requests: The app connects to the content delivery network solely to fetch published articles, metadata manifests, and PDF documents. Standard HTTP web logs (such as IP address and user-agent) may be temporarily processed by content delivery networks to serve files securely.

Offline Storage & Data Control

You have complete control over all data saved on your device. From the in-app Settings screen, you can:

  • Clear downloaded offline PDF files at any time with a single tap.
  • Clear article and image thumbnail caches.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all stored offline files and preferences.

Children's Privacy

SLLK provides historical literature and educational cultural materials. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted here with an updated effective date.
