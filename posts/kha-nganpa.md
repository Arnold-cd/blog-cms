## EPA KHA NGANPA

Eikhoi kangleipak meetei sing gi salai taret (7) lei makhoising adudi Mangang, Luwang , Khuman, Angom, Moilang, Khapa-nganpa, (Ka-nganpa) Salai Leisangthem 

Salai 7 asi kanglei ningthou ahanba houjik ki Imphal kanglada panlamba Konchin Tukthapa Epu Athoupa (Eputhou) Pakhangpa gi machasingni 

Kha-Nganpa haiba kanglei ningthou asi 6 suba macha nupa ni 
Kha-Nganpa bu pokpa leima palem adugi maming na Leitham Tali Leima kouw e 
Ema Ebemma Tali asi Eputhou Pakhangpa gi 6 suba loinabini

Kha -Nganpa na leilampa mafamsing di Imphal gi kangla gi konnungda, mapabung ebungo Pakhangpa ga leiramlaba matung, kha-Nganpa bu khaidoklakpa matamda, kha-Nganpa na ehan hanna tonganna leiramba mafamdi 'Salang Mai ' ni
Salai mai haiba mafam asi houjik ki kangla gi mafam amada oifam thok e
Ayebana mafam asi chap chana takpa ngamdre mathangda kha-Nganpa na Lailelpi Konting Leima ga luhonglaba matungda Nongtamlen Anganpa PoK a 

Matou asum touna meesing hengatlaklaba matamda Kangpokpi da leiramle, Mathangda Langol da leiram me, Mathangda Luwang Chingda Leiram me Ekon Konbada 'Takna Khana Konpa ' mafamda Leiram me

Salai taret ki machu gi manung da Kha-Nganpa ki machu di 'Lumna Nganpa Machu ni 

Eputhou Pakhangpa Konchin Tukthapa Ningthou gi matamda Salai 7 makki thijinlon puya amamam Leiram me makhoising adudi - Mangang lon, Luwang lon, Khumanlon, Angaomlon, Moilang lon, Kha-Nganpa lon, Salai Leisangthem lon asini

Puya mei thabada yaokhre loina mangkhre salailon sing asi fanglabadi salai taret ki matangda houfam satfam leifam salai machu pokpa gi matam ching gi meesing ga tamgi meesing ga leinaba mari, ching gi meesing gi salai asinachingba munna khanglamgadoubani adubu hindu gi matamda puya meithabana tumna manghankhre.
