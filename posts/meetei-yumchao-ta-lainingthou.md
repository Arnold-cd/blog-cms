# MEETEI YUMCHAO TA LAININGTHOU 

Kangleicha meetei eikhoigi thainagi 'meetei yumchao' haina khangnaba leinaba 
yumjao asi ahingba laishangni. Ahingba laishang 'yumchao' asigi manungda yamna maru 
oiba lai ahumgi khupham(leipham) lei.
Ahanba laiphamna, yumchaogi yet thangba nakanda ahanba yum adu urep(jatra) 
makhong lei, masida Lainingthou salailel sitapa (epa sorarel) gi khupham (leipham) ni. 
Mapham asida esaiphu khingani.
Anisuba laiphamna, lainingthou salailel sitapa gi mapa chap chana yumchao gi oi 
thangba nakanda ema leimalel sitapi gi khupham (leipham) lei. Mapham asida leimalel sitapi 
ema gi pheeke phanek,ennaphi thamduna khurumgani. 
Aduga masiha loinana, sanamahee lainingthou gi emung kachingi 
mapangi saruk, moilang laichigi mapangi chithek adubu 'santhong' 
'sanathong' hairaga khangnei. 'Santhong', nattraga 'sanathong' gi 
wahanthok ti mee santhokpa haibani. 
Kangleicha meeteisinggu wangulonda, meeteigi yumchao asi numit, 
tha, thouwaimichak, malem asinachingba achat chatta pumnamak 
yaoriba nongthou, ating-ah, salailel sitapa, epa sorarel (space) asigi 
mahut sinduna sabani.
Eikhoina houjik sijinnariba wahei 'Emung' hairiba wahei asigi 
wahanthok ti 'Ee manung' haibani. Taipang semnaba ee(blood) 
adubu yao e haibani.

EPUNGLOINAPA APAKPA GI SAKTAM(SYMBOL) ꯱•(AMA) ASI KARINO?

Tingpalpa mapu epungloinapa apakpa mahakna taipangpalpa semba pirakpada mapu 
mahakki masak asi "꯱•" (ama) oina utlakpani amasung nupagi saktam louriba 'lainingthou 
salailel sitapa' da mahak haibadi mahakki mahutsinba saktam "꯱•" asibu nachamakni khanna 
'yoklo' haina paotak pirakpani. 
Masigi matungda, lainingthou salailel sitapa na mahakki Masada yaoriba 'sanaki 
masak malpa nouwa ꯱• (ama)' adubu sanamahee mingthol piduna ema leimalel sitapi da 
pisillaga, ema leimalel sitapi na louraga konsinbada 'pali apanpa 
konseng tulei sengpa' sanamahee lainingthou oirakpani.
Puyagi wapham pumnamak asina, houjik eikhoina yumgi sanamahee kachin da 
latliba, khurumliba lai asi 'sanamahee lainingthou' ni haibadi tingpalpa mapu mashak leitaba, 
epungloinapa apakpa mahakni. Saktam(symbol) na saktam ꯱• asini. Eikhoina sanamahee 
lainingthou haiba mamingda khurumbani.

Saktam(symbol) asi karino? 

Eikhoina hanna khangkhare madudi tingpalpa gi mapu, epungloinapa apakpa mahakti 
'talang epung' gi mapu ni haibani. 'Talang epung' haibadi ee(blood) ngaktana talang tarang 
oina leiba haibani. Maram asina saktam ꯱• asina taklibadi tingpalpa mapu epungloinapa 
apakpa aduna taipangpalpa meeoibana chingba uli wali, sa uchek pumnamak puthoknaba, 
semnaba taipangki mapa mahakna saktam ꯱• asi oina taipang da thouwaipanba pumnamak 
semnaba taipang malemda ahanba oina khongdarakpani.

Sanamahee lainingthou gi saktam(symbol) di ꯱• asini. Thainadagi houna sanamahee 
kachin da epa epusingna latlamva saktamni. 
Science ki mityengda sanamahee:
Uli wali, pikkhraba thouwaipanba dagi mee, sa, pumnamak, chaoraba samu phaoba 
thouwai panba achat chatta pumnamak ki Gene(DNA) asi amatani. 
Gene(DNA) amakhaktadagi semgatlakpani, meetei gi thainagi science ki matung 
enna. Amata oiba gene(DNA) adudi meetei na ningsingba ngamdraba matamdagi latlakliba 
sanamahee lainingthou aduni. Saktam(symbol) na ꯱• asini.
Atoppa science ki wapham amana, 
Tingpalpa mapu epungloinapa apakpa na, mahak taipangpalpa semba laklage, numit 
humni suba nouwa ama oina leilamke, haina talang laipao piramliba asina takpadi, malem 
taipangda pallipa, thouwaipalpa pumnamakki thouwaising asi nongmaton thaktagi lakpani, 
Extra-terrestrial origin of life ki mapung phaba lousingni, kangleicha meeteisingna malemgi 
mee pumnamaktagi scientific fact asi hanna khang e haibasini.haibasini.
