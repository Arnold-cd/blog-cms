# ꯄꯨꯌꯥꯒꯤ ꯋꯥꯈꯜꯂꯣꯟ (Wisdom of the Puyas)

The **Puyas** (ꯄꯨꯌꯥ) are the sacred traditional manuscripts of Manipur (ancient *Kangleipak*), written on treated bark (*Agaru* or *Aquilaria agallocha*) and handmade paper using indigenous ink. They encapsulate millennia of historical chronicles, astronomy, philosophical treaties, martial ethics, and botanical medicine.

---

## 1. ꯃꯤꯇꯩ ꯃꯌꯦꯛ: The Script of the Human Anatomy

Unlike alphabets derived purely from abstract pictographs, **Meetei Mayek** is philosophically rooted in human anatomy. The original 18 core characters (*Iyek Ipee*) correspond directly to anatomical components of the human body:

| ꯃꯌꯦꯛ (Letter) | ꯃꯃꯤꯡ (Name) | Meaning / Anatomical Association |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ꯀ** | Kok | Head (ꯀꯣꯛ) |
| **ꯁ** | Sam | Hair (ꯁꯝ) |
| **ꯂ** | Lai | Forehead (ꯂꯥꯢ) |
| **ꯃ** | Mit | Eye (ꯃꯤꯠ) |
| **ꯄ** | Pa | Eyelash / Eyebrow (ꯄꯥ) |
| **ꯅ** | Na | Ear (ꯅꯥ) |
| **ꯆ** | Chil | Lips (ꯆꯤꯜ) |
| **ꯇ** | Til | Saliva / Tongue (ꯇꯤꯜ) |
| **ꯈ** | Khou | Throat (ꯈꯧ) |

This anatomical taxonomy symbolizes the profound indigenous understanding that human consciousness and linguistic communication are inextricably embodied in nature.

## 2. The Great Classical Puyas

Among hundreds of manuscripts preserved by traditional scholars (*Maichous*), several monumental works stand out:

### 1. *Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Amailon Pukok* (ꯋꯥꯀꯣꯛꯂꯣꯟ ꯍꯤꯂꯦꯜ ꯊꯤꯂꯦꯜ ꯁꯂꯥꯢ ꯑꯃꯥꯢꯂꯣꯟ ꯄꯨꯀꯣꯛ)
Considered one of the oldest cosmic philosophy texts of Kangleipak, it expounds on the origin of the cosmos (*Hee-Le-La*), the manifestation of primordial sound, light, and the spiritual evolution of the Seven Clans (*Salai Taret*).

### 2. *Cheitharol Kumbaba* (ꯆꯩꯊꯥꯔꯣꯜ ꯀꯨꯝꯕꯥꯕꯥ)
The Royal Chronicle of the Kings of Manipur. Spanning from 33 CE (the coronation of King Nongda Lairen Pakhangba) to the 20th century, it is one of the longest unbroken written royal annals in Southeast Asia.

### 3. *Hijan Hirao* (ꯍꯤꯖꯟ ꯍꯤꯔꯥꯎ)
An epic philosophical ballad regarding the carving of the first sacred royal boat from a colossal sacred tree, exploring the ecological dialogue and remorse of humanity taking life from the ancient forest.

As our generation transitions toward mobile and cloud architectures, bringing ancient literature into Unicode standards and headless CMS systems ensures that indigenous heritage remains dynamic, searchable, and accessible worldwide.
