Lai haraoba haiba thouram asi karino khanglaktaba.

Adungai Koupru (Koubru) Chingdon da Laiyam singna khunda leitaduna panlamba matamda Tengpalpa Taipangpalpa mapu mahakna Taipang Mee semge haina khanlakle madudagu Mee semba houre ahouba numit adubu Langmaiching kou e mee sembagi Aroiba numit aduna Thangcha kou e.

Kanglei Puya da asumna eri : Talang Paopu Peenapa, Eepungloinapakee Yathangna Oikhipa malamna laiyamsingna 
halaole. Eepungloina Halaole yaifapakee mateekpu laiyamsingna penchale. Aasum Touna Loipee Cheengkee Cheengtonta Mafam, Mafam yengtuna tuuka waaka Patuna Nungngai thoina Pankhale.

Matang asidage Lai haraoba haiba thouram asi lakkhibani. 

Lai haraoba gi matangda :

Lai haraoba haiba asi karino?
Madudi Meeoiba Eikhoina Pokpadagi siba faoba punsi matek asibu lai haraoba kou e , Haibadi pokpana laihou punsi matek aduna lai haraoba, sibana numitpuna lairoi kou e.

Meeoiba Eikhoina harao tayambagi langla matang amada yanduna soifadraba asibabu kaothoktuna hingnari Masibuna Meena haraoba kou e.Lai haraoba haiba kumhei asi meetei wanguba , samna LAINING lambitana tasinduna semlamba kumhei amani.

Asita nattana Meetei Eenat eesel Fijat chakcha loinana sinlon kangleilol chatna kanglon asina chingba suna yaoraba kumhei ni samna hairabada Meetei lai haraoba haiba kumhei aside Meeteibu masak takpa kumhei ni.

Lai haraoba da asumna seisakli 'Sibu thoina haraoba leibara , Leibani  masi Kari hairibano ?
Madudi Meena haraoba thoiba masi nattaba lai na haraoba ama Lei haibane.

Haibadi mapan thong gi Meena haraoriba kumhei asida penthoktuna nunge Wangulon punli haibani Meeoiba eikhoide pal gi kumheida taoduna lei .

Lai haraoba haiba kumhei asibu achangba ama oina loinabatani.Pikliba angang singna lai phadibibu sanaduna harao tayamna  lai phadibi adudi haraode .

Asigumna Meeoiba eikhoi asibu mapu magi sanapotni maram maduna Meeoiba Eikhoina harao tayamna ngaijaktang hingjariba punsi matek asi mapu mana haraohanlibani .

 Meeoiba Eikhoina ngaijaktang haraoriba aside mapu magi sanapotni maram aduna Meeoiba Eikhoina haraoTayamna ngaijaktang hingjariba punsi matek asi mapu mana haraoribani.

 Meeoiba Eikhoina ngaijaktang haraoriba aside mapu mana haraobagi mami aduniMaram Sina Meeoibana Pokpadagi siba tanba faoba punsi matek adubu lai haraoba kou e hairibani.
