chephong (daily paper) kayada sijinnabata nattana, mee pumnamakna khangnaraba 
wafamni. Pao-che singgi matung inna,

Cheihidagi Cheihi (3419-2021=1398) halli haiiba khangi.

Chei matonda sarik yallaga sagol tongduna Pana marigi leirak khullakta "ngasidagi 
houna chahi ama houre" haina laowe haiina eikhoida takpiramliba/tambiramliba amadi 
"chei yaodana atoppa mawongda laoba yadra, kihanbagi mawong nattra" haiiba 
wahang asiga Kanglei Pathou Sairem Nilbir gi warengda 'chei na' 'leipak ningthougi 
matik mangam' takpani haiibasiga punna paminnabada cheiga konnana laobasi 
kihanbani haibasi tasengna khangi. Kihanbaga loinana, meeyamna yaningdaba cheihi 
houbagi mawong ama pusillakpani haiibasi khangi. Konthongda chak 
kat_hanba/thadok_hanba asisu 'cheiraoba' pangthoktaba/ningthou yathang 
indaba/anouba semjinba chatnabi waree asibu yaningdaba mee masak khangdoknaba 
hotnabagi thabakni. Cheihi houbaga konthongda chak thadokpaga mari suklei leinade. 
Meeteigidi Wakchingda Cheihi hou e. 
Poklakkadouriba angang adugi maruna mapada tha ani lei, masibu mapana naopuba 
kou_e. Ahanba thada nupa adugi Wakon, Wakonpung amasung Konching (Kok 
manung) da lei. Wakon, Wakonpung amasung Konching haiiba waheising asidagi Meetei gi ahanba thabu Wakching kou e. 
Wakon chingda leiramba taibang maruna taibang onnabagi mapung faba chaoraktuna 
mamada chatnanabagi sem saduna faiduna leirakpa thagi mamingbu Failel (Fairel) tha 
kou_e. Matam asida mapa oigadaba nupa adugi mamit naha ani longngi. 'Failel' thana 
Meeteigi anisuba Thani.
Puyana meegi (Nupee oibi singgi) masabu 'Leipak Ahingpi' kou_e. Angang maru 
aduna nupigi masada lakpa, tarakpabu lamda tare 'Lamta' tha kou_e. Meeteigi 
ahumsuba Thani. 
Houjikki 'Sajibu' haiiriba wahei asi Meeteigi asengba wahei 'Sachifu' haiba 
waheiidagi ollakpani, hindu matamda ontgokkhibani. Sachifu haibagi 
wahanthokti, Sachifu= Sa+Chi+Fu haiibadi Sa, Huksang (Hakchang), meeoiba 
oirakkadouriba mahee (semen with sperm) + Chi haiibadi chisillakpa, manungda 
changlakpa + Fu haiibadi mafam asida Fu haiiriba asi Chafu gumbani, 
tungnafamni. Mafam asidans nupeegi 'chalot' (womb) puni.
Meeteigi ahumsuba tha Lamta thada nupadagi angang maru yaoba mahi aduna Leipak 
Ahingpi da echai chaina tai. Tariba mahi adudagi 'Tillang' amana nupidagi ovum ga 
tinnaraba matungda, tha taramuk mamagi 'chalot' ta mee onnaba leinaba changsillak e. 
Masibu Kanglei Meetei ,na Sachifu tha kou_e. 
Meeteigi thachatlongi mamingdi meesemlon (embryology) dagi tholli. Meeteigidi 
Cheihi houbasi Wakching thadagi hou_e haiibasi Meeteigi Tha 12 gi wahoulon , wahei 
thoklapagi wahanthok (etymology) nasu tak_e. Sachifu thadi Meeteigi marisuba thani. 
Sachifu (sajibu) thada cheihi, Kum houba sukya yade.

Masina Christagi 18 suba Cheihichagi a_eba khudam ama oina, Meeteidi Cheihi 
(chahi), Kumdi Wakching thadagi da hou_e hyribani. Sachifu (Sajibu) natte haiina 
sengna taklabani. Makhagi Meetei gini haiiriba, hindugi 'sajibu cheiraoba' gi seran 
turan touriba asi yengbiyu:
'Ngariyanbam Kulachandra Singh, pandit loishang, sanakonung' na eramba, 1969 da 
fongba Kanglei Ningthou chahi gi lairikki lamai 2/3 da eramliba asi yengbiyu:
'Mathel lukoi masing yamna thonglaga thongal khudinggi khara fandoktuna, wai 
ningthina teithoklaga heiruk pana thaomei sel numit adugi thongkhiba chaluk 3 
semduna katli, ...katliba thonganda thariba chakluk amata katpasu yao e. Aduga 
chakluk ahum katliba asidi onkhiba chahida cheiraokhiba numit adugi ama, kumsi 
chahigi cheiraoriba numit adugi ama, nongmeigi cheiraogadaba numit adugi ama, 
hairiba numit ahum asigidamakta katlaga, lukkhunbana erammaba tumaba, eram senba 
tusenba, kumsana kumlik lai. Arembana hanuba kokchaone, hannu leikhamne, 
khundahanbane asummakna phangjeiye.' 
Mathakki cheiraobada konthongda thadokpagi thouramda touriba pumnamakse 
khangdabana yenglaba, tarabadi chumlaboi ninglibani, adubu akhangba mee amana 
munna yenglaba, tarabadi chaokhattaba mapuksengdaba mee kangbu amana atoppa meeoiba kangbu amada touba hounadaba, lamchat chatpa adu sengna uiui. 
Mathakta chakluk ahum adubu katliba haiiriba lai ahum adudi: (1) Eeram Maba 
Tumaba (2) Eeram Senba Tusenba (3) Kumsana Kumlik Lai ni. Khangdaba meena 
yengba, taba matamdadi ahumsi lai oiraboi ningba yai. Adubu (1) gi hindu 
cheiraobagi lai 'Eeram Maba Tumaba' haiiba asida yaoriba wahei ahum -
Eeramaba+Tumaba waheising asigi waheigi hourakpham neinaduna yengsi: Eeram 
haiibadi Eelam, haiibadi esing lambi. Maba haiibadi khutna soklaga nanba nattraga 
tapna tapna paiiba. Tumaba haiibasi Maba haiibaduga mannei. Eeram haibasi 
kanagumbana ahanbagi We asi Eesing natte Ee (blood) ni haiina yetlakpa yai. Adu 
oirabadi Meitei cheiraoba gi lai 'eram maba tumaba' haiba lai asibu khongbangumba 
sengba laira nattraga meegi masada pukka suba lai oiba tai?
Anisuba lai 'Eeram Senba Tusenba' haiiba asigisu thoina haiiningai leite. Masisu 
choukidargumna eram khonglam sennaba lai oiba yai?
Ahumsuba lai 'Kumsana Kumlik Lai' haiiba wahei Ani asida yaoriba 'Kum' haiiba 
wahei/concept asigi wahanthok ti hujikki matamda 'chahi' haiiriba wahei aduni, 
karigumba matamda ariba lolda 'cheihi' hainasu sijinnei. 
Kumsana haibana ariba lolda chahi sandokpa haiibani aduga kumlik lai haiibana 
nong chudaba nong likpa chehigi lai haiibani, asigumba cheihida nong chunaba 'nong 
laoba' haiba thouramsu chattharam_e.
Mafam marang khangdana hourakpa manba waheising asina karamna "meitei 
cheiraobagi" laising oirakkhibano? Khanbada mam_e!
Kangleicha Meetei hi oina, Lukhaak pijei haiiriba lai ahum asigi wahanthok 
hourakpham amuk yengbiyu:
(1) Hanuba Kokchao - Tengpalpa semba mapu mahak adu Taibangpal asi mahakna 
sembanina, taipangpalpa asi leitringeidagi mamangda mahakna leirambana, 
taipangpalpagi thouwai panpa pumnamaktagi mahakna ehal halli haiiduna mahakpu 
Kangleicha eikhoina 'Hanupa', 'Hanuba' haiina kou_e. Tengpalpa, Tingpalpa, 
Taipangpalpagi pumnamakta leiriba achat-chatta pumnamak mahakki nungpung 
wakhaldagibu semlakpana, puthorakpana (mahakki KOKtagi thoklakpana) pumnamak 
asi taipang kanagi Kokta khanba ngamdabana, khuwaidagi pak chaona khanba 
ngamba Kok palli haiibadagi mahakki Kokpu 'Kokchao' kou_e. Maram asina 'Hanuba 
Kokchao' haiibadi taipang semba mapu mahakpu haiibani. 
(2) Hannu leikham : Hindu gi matamda 'Hannu Leikham' nattraga 'Hanuba Leikham' 
haiibada yaoriba 'Leikham' haiiba waheisi 'Laikham' haiibani, hindugi matamda 
'Leikham' haiina semdokkhibani. Wahanthok ti - Lai Akhamba, Laii Aroiba haiibadi 
Tengpalpa, Tingpalpa Mapu ( Universal Lord Creator ) haiibani.
(3) Khundahanba - Meeoibabu Taipangpalpa mapuna semlaba matungda, 
taipangpalpa da makhoina ehaan haanna macha masu poktuna pallakpabu khundaba 
nattraga khunthokpa kou_e. Taipangpalbada taibang meeoibana khunthok 
khundadringeida, mapu mahakna makhoibu semlaga khunthok khundahallakpa 
maramna, taipang semba mapu mahakpu Kangleicha singna 'Khunta Ahaanpa' / 
'Khunda Ahaanba' Khundahanba haiina khurumnei. Khunthok khundabadagi mahakna 
halli, haalli haiibani.
Mathakki lai ahum asi malem gi khwaidagi liraba Kangleichasinggi laisingni. Cheihi 
masing khangdana Kangleichasingna khurumduna laklaba laisingni. 'Khundahanba' lai asiga yamna wahanthok naknaba lai amana 'Mongbahanba' lai asini.
Cheihi lising kaya Kangleichasingna khurumjarakpa laisingbu, Christagi 18 suba, 19 
suba cheihichada changlakpa hindusingna, makhoina mafam marang khangdana 
semjinba sajinba laini haiiba kharagi aremmba lukhak chai haiibasi yamna hanthaba 
mawonggi sajatni.
