## KHUMAN

Kanglei meetei epa epu singi puya wakoklon heelel Thilel Salai Ama-elon pukok puya asi, kangleipak ki epa,epu singna hanna khnglamba, malemgi mee atei kanglup na houjikta khangba houriba science ki wafam na pik thalli. Masi nongma nongmagi mayek senglakpagum touri.

Houjik mathakta panjakhiba puya aduda eriba asi yenglagadi Malemgi Space ki manungda machu 7 lottuna lei haibasi ahanba haiba mee kanglup ti kangleicha meetei epa epu singni haibasi khngani.

Adubu 19 suba enggi chahi chada puthokpa chumthang gi machu 7 haibadi Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo and Violet machu taret asiga puyada eba machu 7 ka khetnei. Salai 7 ki ahanba oiriba Mangang, Nula Epema Laikok huimulei puksi khompi na ayuki khonglei machuna ngangpa matamda, NaoSangta pokkhipana, nawa pu mangang mingthol fangcheiye.

Pakhangbagi macha Ahal amadi Anisuba pokpa matam gi machu asi ayukki khonglei machuna Ngangba matamta. Amadi wangna ngouna yungpa khonglei matam haibasi Mangang gi amasung Luwang gi, machu angangba amasung angouba machu louriba asi numit amasung malem anigi leinaba maridagi, mangal dagi Loukhibani.mangaldagi loukhiba machusing ni haiabasi erai laina khang-e.
