> CHINGUTHEL & SANGAITHEL

Sangaithel haiba mafam asi hindu gi matamda  Bhagya Chandra na awana landaraktuna Kangleipak ki konnungda leiba ngamdaduna koichen chenduna lottuna leiba mafamda leiram me haina hainaba .

Nongchup chingjao gi mamang thangba mayaida leiba Kanglei Puwari ga sagolnaba khunfam ama oiba amasung thainagi Kangleipak ki esing nungsit ka mari leinaba wafam kaya Sengna takpa ngamba mafam ni .

Sangaithel haiba mafam asi imphal West ki  sagaolband lambida asum maninglomda thoutharaga patsoi youre .

Patsoi dage khara thoutharaga khongnangbat ama houriba mayada awang thangba maikeida khaidokkhriba lambi macha adu enduna chatlaga tharoijam youre .

 Thouroijam dagi Ching lambi asi thougatlaga houre chingda km. khra thougatlaga sangaithel haiba Meetei Singna taba khul asi youwe .

Masi nongchup thangba Kanglei chingsange napomda leiba leitem manaba mafamni Meetei na chingda leiba leitem manaba tampak machabu thel kou e.

Mafam asi samudra gi Emaidagi meter 1000, muk ti wanglamba yai haina ayeba na thajei imphal tampak ki khuwaidage nemba mafamda gi meter 500, mukti henna Wange .

Matam amada mafam asuwaida chakpa thangja khongjomba na Ningthou oina panlam e .
Mahak ki matam faoba thel asige maming Chinguthel  haina kouram e .

Mahak ki matungda panlamba chakpa konlamba Ningthou gi matamdagi sangaithel haina thel Asige maming Onkhi .

Sangaithel haiba thel Asige maming asi koudouna thonkhibano haiba Asige funga wari lei.

Mafam asida SA ga NGA  ga punna chaktaminnaram e haibage mafam asi sangaithel koukhibani haina HOUKHIBA meerolsing na linarambadagi sangaithel koukhibani haina sangaithel khul gi 2009 da Cheihi  82 suraba Ningthoujam Ahong kouba  ahal amana wari lerak e .

Mathangda Chahi 75 henlaba ahal Ngangbam Kulchand haiba puya paiba ahal amasu ningthoujam ahong gi wari asiga chap mannana lerak e.

Ngangbam Kulchand na chakpa thangja khongjomba amasung Chakpa konlamba na mafam asuwaida Ningthou oiduna panlamba gi wari koupru chingoirol da yaowe haina ayeba da tamlak e.

HOUKHIBA matamda Kangleipak ki esing nungsit karam touram me haibage matangda sangaithel haiba thel Asige sangai haiba wahei machet asi houjik ki matam ge Keibul Lamjao National Park ta leiriba malemge manungda amata ngairaba sa makhal Sangai Brow antlered deer 🦌 asibuni .

Sangai asi esing mapanda leiba semi water dweller animal ni , wafam asimaktana Kanglei tampak ki Puwari gi wafamda cheithatol kumbaba da edi : 1967 gi lamai 122 da Bhagya Chandra na sangaithel da leiram me mathangda Sangaiprou da honglak e haina Sengna eri .

Adubu mafam aduge mee Singna Ningthou amana leirambage khudam amata fanglamde  mafam aduge mee Singna yumjao nattraga  kanagumba Ningthou gumba amana leirambage khudam amta fangde hai .

Ningthoujam ahal asinasu masige matangda pande  mahakna 1980 da mahak ki yumjao adu sanaba gi yumfam thonbada langsoi ama fangkhi haina tam e.

Aduga Ngangbam Kulchand di mahaksu Bhagya Chandra gi matang asi khangsu khangde amta panlamde puya dasu yaode haina tamlak  e.

Bhagya Chandra gi wari asi oiba yaba amdi leiri  sangaithel gi kha nongchup lomda Ngaprum chingjel lei masi umang gi lambida  km. 7 , 8 lappi 

Matam amada Bhagya Chandra na awa kiduna koichel chenduna lotlingaida sangaithel gi umang amasung Ngaprum chingjel maya umang da lottuna leiram e hai .

Ngaprum chingjel gi kangla gi nongchup sanathong yengbada yamna seng oi. Mafam aduwaida leiduna kanglada awa lakpa nattraga chatkhiba yengduna leiram me hai .

Laklaga Cachar da hek chenkhiba awa chatkhraga Kanglei tampak ta kumlaklaga khudongchaba yengduna leiram e  

Sangaithel da langsoi fao e haibasinade samu tongba mee laklam e haibasimadi soidre .

Ching mathakta Ngaprum chingjel kouba mafam leiramba sinade Kanglei tampak ki esing emai asi mafam aduge Ching adu faoba wanglam e haina takpani .
