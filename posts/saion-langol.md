# *'Saion'* nattraga *'Langol'* haiba wahei Animak si amatani ꯫

> Wahei anisina fongdokliba asi amatani potsak amtani ꯫ 

Tengpalpa mapu Eepung loinapa apakpa hairiba aroiba  mapu tengpalpa taipang palpa mapu (Ultimate God Creator Father) adu potsak oina leiba natte wakonpung (Vital Principle, Fundamental concept) oina leibani potsak oina leiba natte wakhal gi mayum wakonpung oina leiba mahakna taipang palpa semlakpa matamda mahak na tengpalpa mapu oirambadagi taipang palpa mapu oirak e ꯫

Taipangpal mapu haibadi eeting tingba karisu yaodaba ahangba Ating aa gi mapu oirambadagi taipang palpa mapu haibadi taipang palpa pokpa, pokpa siba naiba hakchang masa yaoba ahingba potsak (Material body) amagi mapu oirak e
Thouwaimee oirak e ꯫

Mapu magi masige khongchat haibadi wakhal oina leibadagi ahingba thouwai panba potsaksing oirakpa ( from spiritual, conceptual to material living body) asibu Saion Nattraga Langol kou e ꯫

Kangleicha Epa Epusinge wanglaba wangulol ( spiritualism, philosophy) gi wafamni ꯫

Houjik kanglei wahei pukeida (Kanglei Vocabulary) da Saion nattraga Langol haibasi karino yengbiyu ꯫

Saion = Sa + I + on | Saion haina wahei asi Meetei gi Wahei ahumna semba wahei (compound word) ni ꯫

Saion + sa + i + on  haibadi sa, uchek, mee asina chingba  hakchang panba masa yaoba thouwai panbasing haibadi + i haibadi hakchang gi  Ee (i) thouwai panba singe ꯫

i + on haibadi onlakpa oirakpa haina ni , wakhal mayom wakhal chabun (spiritual, immaterial) oirambadagi mapu mana Mee sa uchek na chingba hakchang panba oirakpa ( material body, object) oiramba adubu haibani ꯫

Langol =  lang + on |  langol haiba wahei asi Meetei gi wahei ani na semba wahei ( compound word) ni langol haiba waheina saion nattraga Langol karamna oirakpageoirakpage haibadu otli ꯫

Tengpalpa mapu mahakna matou karamna meeoiba semlakpage haibadu takli

Tengpalpa mapu mahak na taipang meeoiba semba matamda kok na hanna sem e,  Kok ꯀ ki saruktusu laipakna ehan hanna sem e ꯫

Tengpalpa mapu mahak na Meeoiba semnabagi damak paring langsang hunkhale houjik faobada 21 suba cheihe chada yum ama sabada mapu mana paring langsang adugi mayaida leiduna lang gi mawong oina mei esing nungsit leipak Ating aa pumnamak asibu masada chingsinlaga taibang mee gi kok asi semba hourak e ꯫

Mathang mit pa na  ꯃ ꯄ ꯅ ....mapung faba meeoiba gi hakchang semlak e ꯫

Mapu mana ahanba leiba meeoiba semnaba houkhiba mafam adu meeoiba gi Laipak ni  houba numit adubu  Langmaiching kou e, 7 ni changna meeoiba amagi hakchang sembana Thangcha numit ta pumloi loi ꯫
Matou asumna mapu magi wakhalda gi meeoiba ama mapung fana semba adubu langol ba ( langol) kou e ꯫
