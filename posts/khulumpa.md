**ꯈꯨ꯬ꯂꯨ꯬ꯝꯄꯥ**  ****Khulumpa****

Wakoklon heelel thilel salai ama-Elon pukok puya ebada hourakpada hairiba asi yengbiyu. 

" ꯱• Hung, Hei, Ha, Hey " khoiyum Lainingthou" Atigaa koilou sitapa pu khulumna echake.

Mathak ki puyadagi louthokpa wahei pareng asida yaoriba wahei ꯈꯨ.ꯂꯨ꯬ꯝꯅꯥ (khulumna) haiba wahei asida khu, lum haiba haiba wahei machet ani asida "ꯈꯨ꯬" dasu Lum (.) namme, aduga "ꯂꯨ꯬ꯝ" dasu Lum (.) namme. Maram asina mafam asida Eriba khulumna haiba asi eikhoina houjik sijinariba "Khurumba" haiba hindugi matamda sijinramba wahei asidi natte. Houjik eikhoina sijinariba wahei "Khurumba" haibasidi maong kayada pangthok e, Dhandabot touduna khurumba, khut lonnaduna khurumba asinachingba. Maram asina matang asida Aebana haijaningbadi, mafam asida  khulumPa ,KhuLumna puya da sijinariba asidi Kangleicha, meetei sing (maru oina nupi sing) na khurumbagi maong asiga amatani. Khudam oina Lai haraobagi Laimangda "Khuna Leipakta taraga (souraga) leipakta khu souraga khurumba asi khaktani.

Eikhoi kangleicha meetei singna hindugi mamangda dhandabot toubagi chatnabi leirakte. 

Puya da yaoriba khulumpa, Khulumna haiba khurumba maong asi South East Asia da thailand na chingba mafam da chatnei. Mafam asida aebana haijaribadi puya da yaoriba "Khulumna" haiba waheina taklibasi eikhoina houjik chatnariba hindugi matamgi khurumba gi maong asi tasengba kangleicha meeteigi maong natte, kangleicha meetei gidi thailand na chingba mafamda chatnariba maong Khu leitaraga khurumba asini haijabni.
