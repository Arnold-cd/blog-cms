Ngashapa Leisanglel Asang Ngashapa
Matang asida eikhoina samna Ngashapa hairiba asi Kangleipak ki yamna nganleingei gi matam ge Kangleipak ta panlamba Mangang Salai gi Ningthou amage maming ni mahak ki asengba mamingdi Leisanglel Asang Ngashapa haibani Mahak asi Kangleipak ki ahanba Ningthou Konchen Tukthapa Epu Athoupa Pakhangpa gi masu ahan ni

Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok puya da asumna eri " Mapapung Eepung-
ngo Konchin Tukthapa Pakhangpa Sung Fampanthakta, Thachakee Masektakee 
Talakpakumna Meipun Haichao Houpa Oina Nungngai Thoina Pankhale. Aasumna 
Leilapata Lailel Khompeeka Maa-ngang Ningthouka Pannatuna Leisanglel Aasang Ngasapa 
Pokye" Mahak asi Kanglei Puwari da yamna maming chatpa Kanglei Ningthou ni .

Wakoklon Heelel Thilel Salai Ama-ilon Pukok puya gi wari likhul haibadi puya Asige thigatlon wakhallon asi mangang Salai gi Leisanglel Asang Ngashapa hairiba Eputhou Pakhangpa gi masu ahan Ningthou asina puthokpirambani

Puya asida asumna eri " Nana Maichouna Waali Leeye : Chaakpalam Macha Maichouna 
Laikee Chei Thapiye Aatuka Maichou Aahumsu Taapee-ao : 1 Ti Luuwangcham Maachaa Maichou | 2 Ti 
Waahingpam Maacha Maichou | 3 Ti Chinkangpam Maacha maichou | 3 Aashi Yaona 
Kangleiyonta Mayenta Sangkuplei Maakhongta Fampal Langlingeita Waakoklon 1 Ti 
Waahoulon 2 Ti Eenunglon, Khununglon, Aashi Kheipeekkee Waapu Maangang Salai Eeki 
Leisanglel Aasang Ngaasapaa Ningthoukee Waali Leekhunpu Nana Maichouna, Maichou 4 
Ne, Leipak Meeyamne, aashi Kheipeekkee Waapu Maangang Salai Eeki Leisanglel Asang 
Ngasapaa Ningthoukee Waali Leekhunpu Naana Maichoune, Maichou 4Ne, Leipak 
Meeyamne, Asi Kheipeekkee Maafamta Hailakle " ||o||``

Kangla ki sangkuplei makhongta pothaduna famlaga puya gi wari tariba Kanglei Ningthou asi Mungyangpa Ningthou ni houjikti Ningthou Asige Mungyangpa haiba maming asi Cheitharol Kumbaba da Mungyamba haina onthoktuna esinkhre matamsu thengthana esinkhre.
