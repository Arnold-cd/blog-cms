Imphal tampak ki Awang Nongchup pangbada Koupru chingsang gi manung chanba Lok achouba amagi Maming ni 

Sanahal Lokchao asi cheihi lising kaya maning tamna maning tamba kangleipak haiba leipak amagi Mapu oina panlaklaba kangleicha Meetei haiba mee kanglup amagi sanglaba puwari gi chaokhraba awonba amaga mari leinaba Koupru chingsang gi chaoraba lok amagi maming ni 

1709 da pamheiba Garivaniwaz na ahanba hindu ningthou oina kangleipak ki fambal kakhi mahakna chaorakna cheihi 40 muk pankhipankhi
Thangjam Ningol Irom Ongbi thambal (Gomati Maharani) gi macha nupa ahan Irom sanahal na pamheiba Garivaniwaz bu 1748 ta Tanthoklaga Kangleipak ki ningthou oina kangleipak ki fambal kakhi Irom sanahal Mangang salai gi Irom sageida pokpa kanglei ningthou ni

Mahak yam na kuina panba ngamkhdre ningthou Irom sanahal na 1752 da moirang (Moilang) Ningol Maitumbi maru hunduna pokpa samshai ( Shyamjai,Samjai) Khurai-Lakpa na nupi thidana macha ni haina yokkhiba angang Bheygachandra si kangleipakbki tung gi khudong thiba oigadouba mee ni haiba khangduna mahakpu loisinnaba mahakpu thiba lonna konnung thadoktuna chatkhi mahakna lonna konnung thadokpa asi kanglei konnung da leiba pamheiba gi macha saduna yokkhiba mayang macha sing aduna khangkhi 

Matam asida konnung gi thabak asi mama Ebem ma thambal da sennarammi ningthou gi kangbu ama aduga Sai haina taba pamheibagi machasing gi kangbu ama anithokna khaidoktuna Lou yennaduna panba matamni Ningthou sanahal gi kangbu, Yenloumi (Leihoumee) asengba sing asina masing yamlamde 

Konnung da ningthou leitabagi khudongchaba asi louraga Shai haina taba Ningthou gi machasing gi kangbu asina ningthou sendoknaba wa konnung da lan hourak e. thambal gi kangbu amasung Shai machasing asiga akanba lan thoknarakkhi shai kangbu gi luchingba Anantashai lairikyekpam Chanu Haripriyagi machanupa asina luchinglamme Kanglei Chanu asengba thambal maithire
Matam asimaktada ningthou sanahal su hanlakpada mama Ebem ma thambal na ee eetei teina sanakeithenda leiramba thengnare 
Mama ebem madagi konnung gi pao pumnamak tare konnung gi paosina konnung da hanluba kannaroi kanduna ningthou sanahal amasung mama Ebem ma thambal makhoi mathouwaigi damak konnung thadoktuna chenkhi 

Makhoi na Khurkhul da tarakkhi mathangda Koupru chingjao gi sanahal Lokchao da kakhatkhi thoudok asibu kangleicha singna kaoba ngamdaduna lok asibu ningthou sanahal amasung mama ebem ma thambal na kakhiba lokni haiduba matam asidagi ngasifaoba sanahal lokchao haina khangnaduna kouduna lak e.
